## 2. Technical Audit of Core Invariants & Edge Cases

### 2.1 Draw-1 / Draw-3 Semantics, Recycle Order & Undo (`engine.js`, `app.js`)
- **Backward Compatibility**: `createInitialState(shouldShuffle, rngFn, drawMode)` defaults `state.drawMode` to `1` when omitted, keeping all 18 original unit tests passing without modification.
- **Draw & Partial Remainder**: `drawFromStock(state, countOverride)` pushes an undo snapshot and pops `Math.min(count, state.stock.length)` cards to `state.waste` face-up in a single move (`state.moves += 1`), handling `< 3` remaining stock cards without prematurely recycling.
- **Recycle Order Preservation**: When `state.stock` is empty and `state.waste` is non-empty, `drawFromStock` snapshots state and pops all cards from `state.waste` back onto `state.stock` face-down, reversing the waste array so the earliest-drawn cards return to the top of the stock for the next pass.
- **Sticky Draw Mode Across Undo (`DEV-1`)**: `serializeForSnapshot` and `undo` intentionally exclude `drawMode` so a mid-game draw-mode toggle is preserved as a user preference rather than reverted by `undo(state)` (verified by unit test `undo does not revert a mid-game draw mode toggle`).
- **Waste Fan Interactivity**: In `app.js`, `render()` slices up to `fanCount` (`3` in Draw-3, `1` in Draw-1) cards, assigns `--waste-fan-index` (`0..2`), and passes `interactive = isTop` to `createCardElement`, guaranteeing that only the topmost waste card receives click, double-click, and drag listeners.

### 2.2 Hint Engine Priority & Infinite-Loop Prevention (`engine.js`, `app.js`)
- **Read-Only Guarantee**: `findHint(state)` performs zero state mutations.
- **Priority Hierarchy**:
  1. **Foundation (`'foundation'`)**: Checks `state.waste` top card first, then `state.tableau[0..6]` top cards.
  2. **Face-Down Flip (`'tableau-flip'`)**: Checks tableau moves starting at `firstFaceUpIndex(from) > 0` that expose a face-down card.
  3. **Productive Tableau (`'tableau'`)**: Checks waste-to-tableau moves, full face-up stack moves (`idx === 0`) while explicitly skipping King-at-index-0 moves onto empty columns (`state.tableau[toCol].length === 0 && moving[0].rank === 13`), and sub-stack splits (`cardIndex > idx`) only when `from[cardIndex - 1]` is immediately playable to its foundation.
  4. **Stock Draw / Recycle (`'stock'`)**: Falls back to drawing from `stock` or recycling `waste`, or returns `null` when won or no moves remain.
- **DOM Highlighting Lifecycle**: `handleHint()` applies `.hint-source` and `.hint-target` and clears them automatically after `1500ms` or immediately at the start of the next `render()`.

### 2.3 Auto-Complete Gating, Execution & Cancellation Safety (`engine.js`, `app.js`)
- **Gating**: `canAutoComplete(state)` returns `true` iff `!isGameWon(state)`, `state.stock.length === 0`, `state.waste.length === 0`, and every card in `state.tableau[0..6]` is face-up.
- **Step Execution & Simulation**: `autoCompleteStep(state)` selects the lowest-rank eligible card across waste top and tableau tops and delegates to `moveCards(state, best.from, best.to)`, ensuring undo snapshots, move counts, and `+10` foundation scoring apply at each step. `getAutoCompleteMoves(state)` clones state via `cloneStateSnapshot(state)` without mutating live state.
- **Near-Win Deal**: `createNearWinState(drawMode)` deterministically constructs a valid 52-card state (44 cards `A..J` in foundations with `score: 440`, 8 cards `K/Q` on `tableau[0..3]`) that completes in 8 steps to a final score of `520`.
- **Cancellation Safety**: `stopAutoComplete()` clears `autoCompleteTimer`, resets `isAutoCompleting = false`, and re-enables `#btn-auto-complete`, and is called before `handleNewGame()`, `handleNearWinDeal()`, and `handleUndo()`.

### 2.4 Statistics & Timer Lifecycle (`engine.js`, `app.js`)
- **Single-Record Guard**: `recordCurrentGameIfNeeded(won)` is guarded by `gameResultRecorded`, ignores unstarted games (`gameState.moves === 0 && !won`), and prevents a completed game from ever being recorded as a loss (`!won && SolitaireEngine.isGameWon(gameState)`).
- **Resilient Persistence**: `loadDrawModePref`, `saveDrawModePref`, `loadStats`, and `saveStats` wrap all `localStorage` calls in `try/catch` blocks, and `loadStats()` merges stored numeric properties onto `createDefaultStats()`.
- **Timer Accuracy**: Timer starts on the first move (`gameState.moves > 0`), halts immediately in `checkWinState()`, formats via `SolitaireEngine.formatTime(elapsedSeconds)`, and resets cleanly on new or near-win deals.
